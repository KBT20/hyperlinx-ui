# WORKSPACE_RATIONALIZATION_IMPLEMENTATION

Date: 2026-07-03

## Navigation

Workspace navigation now follows the constitutional lifecycle:

- Evidence Intake
- Commercial
- Discovery
- Decision
- Engineering
- Constitutional Truth
- Execution
- Operations
- System

All existing workspace ids remain available. The change is organizational and does not remove capabilities.

## Commercial

Commercial Planning remains the owner of customer, product, commercial design, financial soundness, completeness, and Engineering handoff preparation. Preliminary Proposal is represented as Proposal Readiness under the Commercial group.

## System

Diagnostics-style graph and inventory tools are grouped under System so they no longer compete with lifecycle workspaces.

## Lazy Loading

Workspace bodies are loaded with React `lazy` and `Suspense`, reducing shell load cost and making workspace activation explicit.
